Completion counts and adjustments

How completion is calculated

Road and stretch-aware calculation. Section progress averages applicable Listed, Segmented, Maintenance and General sheets. New roads are excluded. Division and overall progress average section percentages.

Excluded fields: Remarks, Adjacent PWD Sections in Other Districts, and If Other in J Column, Explain never affect completion. Dependent follow-up fields count only when their controlling answer is Yes; No does not make an unrelated neighbouring question optional.

Listed roads
Only Single Stretch = Yes or not yet entered are counted. No moves the road to the segmented target. Progress is filled required fields divided by expected fields; existing conditional field rules remain.
Segmented roads
The parent-road target is the number marked No in Listed roads. A unique matching PRICE Asset ID links stretch entries even when road names differ; otherwise original or corrected names are matched. Each road's progress is the sum of its stretch field-completion fractions divided by its Total Stretches. Repeated road name, asset ID, road class and total-stretch details carry forward only within the same consecutive road group. Stretch-specific details remain required. Duplicate stretch numbers count once; an entered stretch with a blank number receives partial credit but the number remains incomplete. Missing stretches score zero. Parent roads have equal weight. Missing totals use at least one pending stretch (or the highest entered stretch number) and generate a warning.
General road data
One record per unique road name across single and segmented parent roads, including undecided listed roads. Stretches do not multiply road counts. B–I are required. Match original or corrected road names; duplicate names do not add credit.
Maintenance history
Uses the same unique road count as General Road Data. Matches road names in column C against original or corrected names. Each road counts once; C–F and H–M are assessed (10 fields per road). Section Label (B), contact number (G) and Remarks (N) are excluded. Explicit NA, N/A, Nil, None or Not applicable are accepted responses in maintenance-history fields H–M; blanks and dashes remain incomplete.
New roads
Shown for reference only; excluded from all completion totals.

Sheets with no applicable parent roads are N/A and omitted from the average. Missing sheets remain pending. Unmatched segmented/general/maintenance entries are excluded and reported in import notes. Blank and dash do not count as filled; zero does. NA, N/A, Nil, None and Not applicable count only in the maintenance-history fields described above.

Adjustments apply only to the current snapshot; uploading a new ZIP resets them. Uploads are local previews until published. Publish for everyone saves the report and comparison baseline to GitHub. Visitors load the published report and check for updates every minute. Local drafts never override the published report on opening the page.

This measures field filling, not data validity, survey coverage or engineering approval. Prefilled content outside A–C cannot be separated from new entries without a blank baseline.
